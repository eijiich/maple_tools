Notes

  • Only class-relevant stats are increased with Star Force. For instance, a piece of Thief equipment will only have their Luck and Dexterity increased with each Star Force level.

Weapons

When increasing a weapon's Star Force, Attack Power and Magic Attack will only increase if the weapon had a non-zero base value of it. For instance, Daggers will only receive bonuses to Attack Power since they do not have any innate Magic Attack.
