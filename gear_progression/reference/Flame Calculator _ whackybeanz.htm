Extras

  • If you do not see Steps 3, 4 and 5, create an equipment setup.
  • Individual flame tier chances and possible flame lines are retrieved from the probability data supplied by Korea MapleStory.
  • You can use Vexilar's Chrome or Firefox Extensions to more conveniently swap between different equipment setups.
  • You can use MapleScouter (Efficiency - Boss Cut) or MathBro's Stat Equivalent Calculator to calculate stat equivalence values in step 3.
  • You can use Lukishi's Flame Distribution Calculator to more easily select your equipment's flame stat distribution in step 4.
  • There will be a slight pause when you save data in step 4. This is due to the probability calculation running (there's a lot of permutations). Thanks for your patience!
  • Special thanks to Pastel, Congel, Kagari, Steph, minmin, aced and bloctopus for bearing with me and my random math questions on probability and coding when I needed it the most.
  • If you have any queries, suggestions or feedback, please contact me on my Discord server.
